# Devil n Dove — Release 467 Build 462: Canadian Product Safety & Label Operator Review

## Purpose and hard limit

Private, low-cost, **read-only** operator worksheet at `/admin/maker-product-safety/`. It uses a single explicit existing Product Admin GET, then optionally reads existing Maker Owner Trial history for an exact Creative Project and journey. There is **no migration, D1 write, new provider dependency, auto-classification, compliance badge, product-status change, store publication, shipment or Etsy/Meta write**. Downloaded JSON is an *unapproved HOLD worksheet*, not a server receipt or legal/physical safety certificate.

This build **does not approve or release any real Product**. Category and intended use must be determined by a responsible person from packaging, advertising, intended users and foreseeable use. Mixed or special-use products may require more than one regulatory regime. Private learning/therapeutic work has no obligation to become commercial Product stock.

## Source-linked operator path

1. Open the original Product Editor. Find exactly one real Product ID. In the new worksheet enter that ID, explicitly press **Load exact saved Product**, and compare returned name/category/status with the physical item. A stored category is a hint, never legal classification. Missing Product or a mismatched ID stays HOLD.
2. Choose one category deliberately: unclassified; adult jewellery; jewellery directed primarily at under-15s; candles; soaps/cosmetics; plush/toys; resin/polymer/fabrication; other. Unclassified/other necessarily requires specialist classification. For mixed goods repeat the applicable reviews; do not use the general category to erase higher-risk requirements.
3. Compare each applicable current government source, examine actual materials/lots, workmanship/QA, package/label, warnings/claims and physical test evidence. Check a question only after personal review and identify an **original** QA/lot/test/label reference. A checked item is not a legal pass or certifiable laboratory result.
4. If there is a genuine linked Creative Project, enter its exact ID and owner-trial journey; deliberately press **Check existing immutable Maker Owner Trial receipts**. The source is `/api/admin/maker-owner-trials` and remains canonical. Use the separate Owner Trial interface to record `physical_safety` linked to an actual traveler or QA source, personally observed and with honest HOLD/accepted observations. Receipt acceptance means human *source review* only; it does not connect the Product to the project automatically, establish finished-product compliance or certify anything. Confirm the actual Product-to-project relationship independently.
5. Download the optional `HOLD` JSON worksheet for owner files (local browser download only). It identifies original Product, optional project, category, manually typed evidence references, official links and explicit open conditions. It creates **no** immutable server receipt. The original Maker Owner Trial ledger remains the durable evidence authority.
6. Open Packaging Studio/Label Production and relevant original QA, Inventory, finished-piece and Product records. Manually compare the actual physical printed label. Consult qualified guidance where the law, testing, classifications or claims are uncertain. **Keep product/publication on HOLD** until all independent gates are genuinely completed elsewhere.

## Current official Canadian starting points (reviewed October 24, 2026)

- [Health Canada — Canada Consumer Product Safety Act industry guidance](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/consumer-product-safety/reports-publications/industry-professionals/canada-consumer-product-safety-act-guide.html) and [incident reporting](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/consumer-product-safety/legislation-guidelines/acts-regulations/canada-consumer-product-safety-act/industry/guide-mandatory-reporting-section-14.html): general consumer-product risks, truthful claims and incident duties.
- [Competition Bureau — mandatory label information](https://competition-bureau.canada.ca/en/labelling/prepackaged-non-food-consumer-products/what-must-be-label): product identity, net quantity, dealer identity and applicable language/exception/format requirements for covered prepackaged non-food goods. Verify coverage and exemptions for each item.
- [Health Canada — candle safety](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/healthy-living/your-health/products/candle-safety.html): real burn tests and warning/use review remain human/physical tasks.
- [Health Canada — cosmetic labelling](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/consumer-product-safety/reports-publications/industry-professionals/labelling-cosmetics.html), [what is a cosmetic](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/cosmetics/what-is-cosmetic.html), and [cosmetic notification](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/consumer-product-safety/cosmetics/notification-cosmetics.html): handmade body soaps generally fall under cosmetics; the rules are claim- and product-dependent. Manufacturers/importers generally notify within **10 days of first Canadian sale**. Notification is not approval.
- [Health Canada — children's jewellery](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/consumer-product-safety/reports-publications/industry-professionals/guide-children-jewellery/guidance-document.html): applies to jewellery marketed/designed primarily for children under 15, with specific component lead/cadmium limits and testing. No blanket adult-jewellery assumption for children's products.
- [Health Canada — Toys Regulations guidance](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/consumer-product-safety/reports-publications/industry-professionals/industry-guide-safety-requirements-children-toys-related-products-summary/guidance-document.html) and [Competition Bureau — stuffed/filled textile labelling](https://competition-bureau.canada.ca/en/labelling/textile-labelling/guide-labelling-stuffed-or-filled-textile-articles): soft-toy flammability, component hazards, coatings and textile/filling labelling where applicable; verify exclusions. A **3+ or collector label is not by itself proof of exemption**.

The sources are guides, not substitutes for applicable up-to-date legislation, tested physical evidence, a product-specific regulatory determination or legal advice. Do not claim a Health Canada certification: the CCPSA does not confer such product certification.

## Technical acceptance, separate from real owner acceptance

- Pure policy always returns `HOLD_CANADIAN_SAFETY_OWNER_REVIEW` and `certified:false`, `labelApproved:false`, `publicationAllowed:false`, no writes, even if synthetic questions/owner receipts all appear complete.
- Correct Product identity is required before displaying source-review controls. Replacing a Product or category invalidates temporary checklist state. Switching Project/journey invalidates old receipt display. Source proof is bounded (single Product lookup, explicit optional original owner-trial GET).
- Synthetic tests cover category coverage, source domains, stale/mismatched Product and trial scope, superseding HOLD receipts, and keyboard/mobile interface with no POST/storage mutation.
- Current application quality gate and exact-source Development/Production promotion gates must pass. Production GREEN means the worksheet shipped; it **never means a candle, cosmetic, plush, jewellery piece or label is safe/certified for sale**.
