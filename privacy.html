Devil n Dove policies

Privacy Policy

How Devil n Dove collects, uses, protects, and handles website, order, media, and social-platform information.

Ontario, CanadaLast updated July 12, 2026

Information we collect

We collect information people choose to provide when they create an account, place an order, request a custom item, contact us, join a waitlist, submit media, or connect a social account. This can include names, contact details, shipping or pickup details, order information, account identifiers, and messages.

Payment-card details are handled by the selected payment provider and are not intended to be stored directly by Devil n Dove.

Website and device information

Our hosting, security, analytics, and commerce services may process IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, referral information, timestamps, cookies, and security events. We use this information to operate the site, prevent abuse, understand performance, and improve products and content.

Social-platform connections

When an administrator connects Facebook, Instagram, Pinterest, X, TikTok, or YouTube, the platform may provide account identifiers, granted permissions, access and refresh tokens, token-expiry information, Page or channel identifiers, and publishing results. We use those details only to provide the requested connection, draft, publishing, and reporting features.

We do not ask for or store social-platform passwords.

How we use information

  • Fulfil orders, pickup, shipping, custom requests, refunds, and customer support.
  • Operate member, inventory, catalog, media, CAIP, and approved social-publishing workflows.
  • Protect the site, investigate failures, prevent fraud, and maintain audit records.
  • Improve navigation, accessibility, search visibility, and product presentation.
  • Meet legal, tax, accounting, and platform obligations.

Sharing and service providers

Information may be processed by services that help us host the website, store data and media, process payments, deliver messages, analyze performance, or connect social platforms. We disclose only what is reasonably needed for the service. We may also disclose information when legally required or to protect people, property, and the integrity of the service.

Retention and security

We retain information only as long as reasonably needed for the purpose collected, legal obligations, dispute handling, security, and business records. Social tokens should be encrypted and revoked when disconnected. No system can promise absolute security, but we use access controls, encrypted secrets, audit logs, and least-privilege design.

Your choices

You may ask to access, correct, or delete eligible personal information, withdraw consent where applicable, or disconnect a social-platform connection. Some transaction and security records may need to be retained. See our Data Deletion Instructions.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests can be sent through the Devil n Dove contact page. Please do not include passwords, access tokens, payment-card numbers, or unnecessary sensitive information.

This policy describes the current intended operation of Devil n Dove. Platform-specific authorization screens may provide additional details at the time permission is requested.
