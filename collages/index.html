Explore Devil n Dove through Product collages

Collages are visual arrangements of current approved public Product photography. They help you discover pieces by sight while every image still links back to its real Product, price, inventory and buying path.

A visual layer, not another catalog

Same Product truth

Same approved photography

Collages use the already public-media-gated Product image projection rather than reading private media directly.

Responsive discovery

The mosaic reshapes for Web, Phone and Desktop and honors reduced-motion preferences.

How Collages will grow

Release 448 establishes the reusable presentation authority. Future published collage presets can point at a Collection and choose mosaic, feature-grid or story-strip layouts without copying Product records or image files. Photography Manager recommendations can later help an administrator choose strong candidates, but they do not automatically replace Product media.
