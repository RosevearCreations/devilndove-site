THE LEGEND OF DOVLIN
Previously approved narration; retain this wording for the canonical story section.

Long ago, in a little workshop where imagination had no boundaries, unfinished treasures waited patiently for their chance to become something wonderful.

One evening, a wild spark of mischief danced across the room and met a single golden feather. For one breathtaking moment, fire and kindness became magic.

There was a flash. A tremendous pop! And straight into a basket of fabric scraps fell a creature with curved horns, little wings, enormous brown eyes, and a most suspicious smile.

His name was Dovlin.

He spilled paint, tangled thread, misplaced tools, and even glued his tail to the workbench. Twice. Yet every mistake inspired another idea, another laugh, another try.

Then one quiet night, Dovlin found a tiny golden dove pendant. As it glowed, a gentle whisper told him: A little mischief makes life interesting. A little kindness can change someone's world.

Dovlin put the dove around his neck and made a promise. He would keep creating, keep laughing, and always try to make things better.

Because you don't have to be perfect to have a beautiful heart.

And so the legend began.

Dovlin: a little mischief, a whole lot of heart.
