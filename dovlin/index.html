Before the first stitch

Care, safety & making notes

Can we buy these editions now?

Not yet. All ten plush images, workshop ideas, and additional palettes are Concept Previews and In Development. No inventory, prices, release dates, or reservations are established here.

Who are the concepts designed for?

The planned plush format is approximately 8 inches, intended for ages 3+ and adult collectors. No final safety, age-label or physical quality assessment is claimed.

What about wood, resin and molds?

The CNC board designs are negative pockets with natural wood grain visible beneath transparent, flush-finished resin and a dark stained surround. These are decorative display concepts until food-contact materials are validated. Resin, candle and skin-contact soap molds need separate material testing. No STL, G-code or production embroidery files are included.

Are the medallions genuine coins?

No. They are original custom decorative non-currency tokens, not altered legal-tender coins. Full-colour tile and canvas finishes require printing or painting processes; a laser alone cannot create arbitrary full-colour artwork.

Are all edition colours approved?

The planned names are retained. Burgundy Shadow uses provisional Shadow Ruby imagery, Ivory Dove uses provisional Gold Edition imagery, and Silver Moon uses provisional Moonstone imagery; each needs owner visual approval before a product listing.
