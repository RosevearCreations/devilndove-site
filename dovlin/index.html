Small print, big care

Care, production and safety notes

Can we purchase these plush characters today?

No. These are illustration concepts and planned ideas, not manufactured products or verified inventory. There are no prices, deposits, scheduled launches or Buy buttons.

Are these toys tested or certified?

No physical toy, safety-test result or final compliance claim is supplied. The proposed eight-inch plush has an intended future audience of ages 3+ and adult collectors; final classification, sewn construction, permanent golden dove detailing, labeling and any required testing must be completed before selling.

Do the concept pictures include finished CNC files or molds?

No printable STL, G-code, embroidery program or finished sewing pattern is supplied. CNC decorative boards depict a negative pocket with visible wood grain below flush crystal-clear resin and dark-stained surroundings; do not represent a food-safe cutting surface. Resin, skin-contact soap and candle molds need separately validated materials and processes.

Are the coin illustrations real money or legal tender?

No. They describe original custom non-currency token/medallion concepts. Full-colour tile and canvas artwork requires printing, sublimation, paint or another finishing process rather than laser engraving alone.

What does the golden dove stand for?

Kindness, compassion and making something better. The wearable-looking gold dove in artwork is a design symbol; a future plush would require a safe, permanently integrated sewn or embroidered detail, not a loose charm.

Every image belongs to the owner's supplied concept handoff. Image colourway studies do not replace or modify the original mascot lines and personality.
