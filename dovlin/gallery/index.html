What must happen before a concept becomes a real product? A maker must inspect the physical sample and measurements, record actual materials and their source, review the character artwork against the approved original, assess applicable safety and care requirements, authorize imagery and approve a real SKU and stock quantity. None of these requirements is automatically satisfied by a design image.
