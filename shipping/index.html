Shipping & delivery

We ship eligible physical orders within Canada and offer local pickup. The shipping amount is calculated again at checkout before payment.

Canada-only online shipping · CAD · Policy published November 25, 2026

Shipping destinations and cost

Canada only. We currently do not accept United States or international shipping orders.

Standard parcel shipping: $15.00 CAD per shipped order, before applicable tax. This is the current storefront checkout charge for physical orders that require shipping, not a free-shipping promotion. Multiple eligible items in a shipped order use the checkout's single flat shipment charge.

Products fulfilled digitally do not require parcel shipping. Local pickup has no parcel shipping charge; pickup time is arranged individually.

Dispatch and carrier delivery

For items shown as ready to ship, allow approximately 5–10 business days for handling before handoff to a carrier, plus approximately 3–15 business days for carrier transit within Canada. These are service estimates, not guaranteed arrival dates.

Weekends and statutory holidays are not business days. Remote addresses, carrier disruption, weather, stock verification and workshop circumstances may cause delays. Contact us for an updated estimate before ordering if a date is important.

Handmade, made-to-order and fragile pieces

Custom or personalized work can require additional production time; the relevant quote or listing should confirm those arrangements before payment. If an item cannot meet the ordinary handling estimate, we will contact the purchaser with an updated option.

Packaging and shipping service are selected for the actual item. Customers will receive shipping or pickup instructions when arranged. We do not claim guaranteed tracking or automatic delivery if a carrier has not supplied it.

Changes, problems or delivery questions

Check the shipping address carefully at checkout. Contact us promptly for a correction before dispatch; changes may not be possible after carrier handoff.

If a parcel arrives damaged, is incorrect or has not arrived within the communicated estimate, contact Devil n Dove with the order reference so we can investigate. See returns and refunds for the next steps.
