Shop Devil n Dove by collection

Collections are Storefront merchandising views over the same public Products and approved Product images used by the Shop. They help separate workshop-made work from vintage, antique, collectible, oddity and found stock without creating a second catalog.

One Product authority, clearer shopping paths

A Product remains a Product whether it appears in Shop, a Collection or a Collage. Collection membership changes presentation and discovery only; it does not duplicate price, inventory, Product photography or checkout truth.

What Collections control

Name, public description, ordering, optional rules and explicit Product membership. A Collection can group Products by origin/category or be manually curated.

What Collections never own

Product price, inventory quantity, Product image binaries, Product SEO truth, checkout state or manufacturing claims stay in their existing authorities.
