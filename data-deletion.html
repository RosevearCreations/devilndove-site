Data Deletion Instructions

How to request deletion of eligible Devil n Dove account, media, and connected social-platform information.

Ontario, CanadaLast updated July 12, 2026

Request deletion from Devil n Dove

  1. Open the contact page.
  2. Choose or write “Data deletion request.”
  3. Provide the email address or account identifier associated with the request.
  4. Identify whether the request covers a Devil n Dove account, order/contact records, uploaded media, or a social-platform connection.
  5. Do not send passwords, tokens, or payment-card information.

We may ask for reasonable verification before deleting or releasing account information.

Disconnect a social platform

Use the platform's own Apps and Websites or connected-app settings to revoke Devil n Dove access. Revocation stops future API access but may not delete posts already published. You may also ask us to remove stored connection tokens and identifiers.

  • Meta: Facebook/Instagram Accounts Center or Business Integrations.
  • Pinterest: Settings → Security and logins → Apps.
  • X: Settings → Security and account access → Apps and sessions.
  • Google/YouTube: Google Account → Security → Third-party access.
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Manage app permissions.

What may be retained

We may retain limited transaction, tax, fraud-prevention, security, audit, consent, dispute, or legal records when required or reasonably necessary. Where full deletion is not permitted, access can be restricted and the information minimized.

Expected handling

We will acknowledge a complete request and provide a status or ask for needed verification. Timing depends on the request, account verification, backups, legal obligations, and connected services.
