Social Platform Connection Disclosure

How Devil n Dove uses authorized Facebook, Instagram, Pinterest, X, TikTok, and YouTube connections.

Ontario, CanadaLast updated July 12, 2026

What connecting means

A connected platform authorizes Devil n Dove to perform only the scopes approved on the platform's consent screen. We do not receive the social account password. Connections are used for Devil n Dove-owned Pages, professional accounts, boards, profiles, or channels.

Review-first publishing

Eligible products may create internal draft packages, but drafts remain unpublished until reviewed. Media rights, privacy, product facts, destination, caption, links, and platform status must be checked before publishing.

Data handled

The integration may store provider name, account and Page/channel identifiers, granted scopes, encrypted tokens, expiry times, connection status, post identifiers, timestamps, errors, and aggregate performance results. Tokens must remain server-side.

Disconnecting

Connections can be revoked through the social platform and, when the in-app controls are completed, through Devil n Dove. See Data Deletion Instructions for removal requests.

Registered callback URLs

These endpoints are present so provider dashboards can validate exact HTTPS redirect locations. They do not publish content by themselves.
