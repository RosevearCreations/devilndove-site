Returns & refunds

We want you to understand what can be returned before you order. The return method and cost depend on whether the item is simply unwanted or has a problem.

Canada-only online shipping · CAD · Policy published November 25, 2026

14-day return request window

For eligible, ready-made standard items bought directly from Devil n Dove, contact us within 14 calendar days after delivery or collection to request a return. Do not send an item back without first arranging the return with us.

To qualify for a change-of-mind return, an item must be unused, unaltered, undamaged and in the condition in which it was delivered, with included components and packaging where practical. We will explain the return instructions when the request is reviewed.

Return shipping, fees and refund timing

For an approved change-of-mind return, the buyer pays the actual return postage charged by their selected postal or courier service. There is no separate restocking fee. We cannot quote a fixed return postage amount without the origin, size and carrier.

If we supplied an incorrect, defective or materially misdescribed item, contact us: we will arrange a fair remedy and cover reasonable required return postage where applicable. Approved refunds are normally initiated to the original payment method within 10 business days after the returned item is received and reviewed; banks or card issuers may take additional time. Original outward shipping may not be refunded for a voluntary change-of-mind return, subject to applicable law.

Custom and hygiene exceptions

Made-to-order or clearly personalized items, delivered digital files and opened personal-care or hygiene goods are not ordinarily eligible for a voluntary change-of-mind return. This does not remove statutory rights for goods that are defective, incorrect, unsafe or not as described.

Vintage, antique, collectible and one-of-a-kind standard items may be returned within the stated window if they remain in their original described condition and meet the other eligibility rules. Document known wear and condition before ordering.

How to start a return or resolve an issue

  1. Open our contact form and provide the original order reference and whether the issue is a return, exchange, damaged item or incorrect item.
  2. Describe the concern; include photos where relevant without publishing personal information.
  3. Wait for the return instructions and destination before mailing anything. Keep postage and tracking receipts if available.
  4. Once the item or evidence is reviewed, we will confirm the appropriate refund, replacement, repair or other remedy.

Returns are reviewed by a person; submitting a request does not automatically charge, refund or initiate a carrier booking.

Legal consumer rights

This policy concerns ordinary voluntary returns for direct Devil n Dove orders in Canada. It does not override mandatory rights or remedies under applicable Canadian and provincial consumer law. Orders completed through an external marketplace must also follow the relevant marketplace's applicable terms.
