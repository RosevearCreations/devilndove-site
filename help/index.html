Help for shoppers, members and creators

Devil n Dove Help Centre

Use the circular ⓘ controls throughout the site for help beside the method or process you are using. This page collects the most common explanations in one place.

Shopping and Product details

  • Shop by intent is a discovery shortcut over the same Product records. It does not create duplicate listings.
  • Advanced Product Search combines filters such as origin, type, availability, material, process, locality and price.
  • Product details show current public Product information, images, price, availability and supporting workshop facts.
  • Cart and checkout verify current pricing, quantity and fulfilment rules again before an order is completed.

Open Shop

Product images

Devil n Dove Product cards and Product detail pages show the complete uploaded image by default. Portrait and landscape photographs may have intentional empty space around them so the Product is not cut off.

  • The Storefront does not silently crop Product uploads to make every card look square.
  • If a Product image needs an intentional crop, resize or re-centering decision, that work is done in the Product Media & Image Editor by an authorized creator or administrator.
  • A focal point records the important subject position for intentional presentation work. It does not automatically crop the public image.

Wishlist, back-in-stock and availability

  • Wishlist saves a Product for later and does not reserve stock.
  • Back-in-stock follows availability for a Product that cannot currently be purchased.
  • Local pickup information explains pickup eligibility and expectations; checkout still confirms the final fulfilment choice.

Creators and workshop workflows

Creative Projects

Track the making process, materials, evidence and content opportunities around a piece or experiment.

Media & Content Studio

Manage non-Product workshop, process, gallery, proof, banner and engagement photography. Product gallery images remain in the Product Media editor.

Content Studio

Prepare reviewed stories, captions and publication packages from approved evidence. Preparation does not publish automatically.

Packaging Studio

Prepare labels, packaging layouts and repeatable templates from approved Product and ingredient facts before final print/use.

Using contextual help

The circular ⓘ control beside a term or method explains what it means, what it changes and what it does not change. The floating ⓘ Help control returns to this Help Centre.

Help is explanatory and read-only. Opening help never saves data, changes Inventory, edits media, publishes content, places an order, or calls a payment/provider action.

Need more help?

For a question about a Product, order, pickup, custom request or website behavior, use Contact and include the page or Product name when possible.
