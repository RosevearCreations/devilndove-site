About Devil n Dove

Devil n Dove is our workshop, our creative outlet, and our way of keeping life moving—especially on the days that don’t cooperate. We’re not chasing perfection. We’re chasing progress, curiosity, and the kind of making that feels like breathing again.

What we do

  • Jewelry, mixed media, and small-batch creations
  • 3D design, printing, CNC, carving, and casting experiments
  • Laser engraving, sublimation, and “what else can we try?” projects
  • Behind-the-scenes videos: the wins, the fails, and the laughs

Why we do it

Making is therapy here. It’s structure, focus, and a reason to show up. We work around real limitations—and we share that honestly—because it’s part of the story and part of the strength.

If you’ve ever used creativity to stay grounded, you’ll get it. If not—welcome anyway. Pull up a chair.

What you’ll find on this site

  • Art — finished pieces, experiments, and collections
  • Tools — what we use, what we recommend, and what surprised us
  • Shop — items you can buy when they’re available
  • Movies — our personal library, just for fun

Sourcing, provenance, and condition

Devil n Dove is not only handmade work. We also sell vintage pieces, oddities, old tools, collectibles, and pre-built items. When something is not workshop-made, we want that stated plainly with sourcing notes, condition summaries, era clues, and outside-listing links where needed.

  • Handmade items focus on process, materials, and workshop context.
  • Vintage and collectible items focus on provenance, wear, patina, testing, and what is known versus unknown.
  • Odd tools and found items can link out to Facebook Marketplace or other sales channels when that is the clearest way to sell them.

How sales channels work

Some items are sold directly on Devil n Dove, some are listed both here and elsewhere, and some are external-only listings. We keep that visible so buyers understand whether they are purchasing a workshop-made piece, reserving a collectible, or following a link out to another marketplace.
