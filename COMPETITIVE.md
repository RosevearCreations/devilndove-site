# Competitive Roadmap — Devil n Dove

For mixed media artists, handmade shops, vintage/collectible sellers, and small creative businesses.

## Executive takeaway

Devil n Dove should not feel like a generic storefront. The strongest competitive position is: **a real Southern Ontario workshop where handmade, mixed-media, vintage, and experimental creations each carry a story, process proof, and honest human context.**

The site should compete by being more useful, more personal, and easier to trust than a marketplace listing. The winning direction is not just “sell products.” It is:

1. Show what we make.
2. Show how we make it.
3. Explain what it is made from.
4. Explain why it is one-of-a-kind, small-batch, vintage, sourced, custom, or experimental.
5. Make buying, pickup, sharing, and asking for custom work easy on a phone.

## Competitive positioning

### What Devil n Dove should be known for

- Handmade mixed-media jewelry and gifts.
- Polymer clay earrings and small-batch wearable art.
- Coin rings, spoon rings, stainless steel pieces, and metalwork experiments.
- Laser engraved and personalized gift ideas.
- Sublimation and 3D-designed workshop pieces.
- Vintage, collectible, and sourced finds with honest condition notes.
- Southern Ontario maker-shop personality.
- Process-first content: experiments, oops moments, finished pieces, shop stories, and therapy-workshop creativity.

### What we should not try to be

- A mass-production jewelry store.
- A generic Shopify catalog with no maker story.
- A polished luxury brand that hides the workshop reality.
- A marketplace dump with no condition, provenance, size, or care notes.

### The strongest brand promise

**Workshop-made and story-backed gifts, jewelry, art experiments, and vintage finds from Southern Ontario.**

## Priority roadmap

### Tier 1 — Must-have competitive foundations

1. **Story-first homepage**
   - Make the hero instantly say what Devil n Dove is.
   - Recommended main message: “Handmade Mixed Media Jewelry, Gifts, Art & Vintage Finds.”
   - Add buttons for Shop, How We Make Things, Vintage Finds, Custom Gifts, and Local Pickup.

2. **Product story blocks**
   - Every product should have a short story section.
   - Include what it is, how it was made or sourced, what materials were used, and what makes it different.

3. **Media readiness score**
   - Track required images: main, detail, scale, back/side, process, and optional video.
   - Keep image alt text and captions mandatory for publish readiness, not draft creation.

4. **Phone-first buying path**
   - Keep the compact menu.
   - Add quick chips for Shop, Search, Cart, New Arrivals, Under $25, Local Pickup, and One-of-a-Kind.

5. **Local trust block**
   - Add reusable Southern Ontario/Tillsonburg/Oxford/Norfolk wording naturally.
   - Include local pickup info where appropriate.

### Tier 2 — Strong conversion improvements

6. **Gift-intent collections**
   - Under $25 Gifts
   - One-of-a-Kind Pieces
   - Gifts for Makers
   - Gifts for Gamers / Fantasy Fans
   - Personalized Engraved Gifts
   - Local Southern Ontario Gifts
   - Vintage Finds
   - Workshop Experiments

7. **Better product filters**
   - Product type
   - Material
   - Colour
   - Technique
   - Theme
   - Price
   - Handmade / vintage / sourced
   - Ready to ship
   - Made to order
   - Local pickup available

8. **Vintage and sourced item proof**
   - Add fields for condition, era estimate, source story, flaws, measurements, and handling/cleaning notes.

9. **Custom request flow**
   - Simple intake for engraving, personalization, ring ideas, or “can you make something like this?”

10. **Testimonials and proof**
   - Store testimonials privately first.
   - Approve before public display.
   - Connect suitable testimonials to product categories and local pages.

### Tier 3 — Growth and automation

11. **Search Console action loop**
   - Import Search Console CSV data.
   - Generate private title/meta/internal-link tasks.
   - Review before editing public pages.

12. **Social content pipeline**
   - Queue process photos and summaries.
   - Use platform-specific captions.
   - Dry run before posting.
   - Add UTM links for measurement.

13. **Marketplace export readiness**
   - Prepare safe exports for Etsy/Facebook/Pinterest/manual channels.
   - Never expose private Amazon costs/order data in public files.

14. **Margin visibility**
   - Show rough product cost, fees, shipping, and margin before publishing or discounting.

15. **Event/market mode**
   - Build QR product cards, quick inventory lists, manual-sale capture, and post-event social recap flow.

## Product page blueprint

Every product page should eventually include:

- One clear H1 product name.
- Short subtitle explaining what it is.
- Price and stock status.
- Ready-to-ship or made-to-order state.
- Product type and collection.
- Handmade, vintage, sourced, or custom label.
- Material list.
- Size/dimensions.
- Weight where helpful.
- Colour/finish.
- Care notes.
- Shipping/pickup note.
- Return/final-sale note.
- The Story Behind This Piece.
- Process/making notes.
- Condition/provenance notes for vintage finds.
- Image gallery with main/detail/scale/process media.
- Social share caption starter.
- Related pieces or collections.

## Mobile UX direction

The mobile site should prioritize fast taps:

- Menu should stay compact and grouped.
- Shop/Search/Cart should be obvious.
- Product cards should have clear title, price, image, and stock status.
- Filters should collapse cleanly.
- Admin panels should avoid endless vertical walls.
- Forms should show only draft-required fields first and move polish fields into readiness checklists.

## Social platform direction

### Current safe strategy

Use the Social Posting Queue as the centre of the workflow:

1. Capture job/process photo or finished item photo.
2. Create a review-first caption.
3. Add platform-specific caption variants.
4. Dry run the platform payloads.
5. Approve the post.
6. Manually post or API publish only when platform credentials are ready.
7. Store the public post URL and UTM campaign.

### Platform notes

- Facebook and Instagram can be supported through Meta APIs once Page/Instagram professional account permissions and tokens are configured.
- TikTok needs app setup, scopes, and media/domain rules before direct posting.
- X posting may have cost/API-tier concerns, so keep manual/copy mode as a safe fallback.
- YouTube Shorts/Community should remain future/manual until upload handling and OAuth flow are properly built.
- Pinterest is a good fit for finished products, gift guides, and evergreen workshop inspiration.

## Local SEO direction

Continue building around:

- Handmade gifts in Southern Ontario.
- Handmade jewelry in Southern Ontario.
- Polymer clay earrings in Ontario.
- Custom laser engraved gifts in Ontario.
- Vintage finds and collectibles in Ontario.
- Local pickup around Tillsonburg/Oxford/Norfolk where accurate.

Do not stuff location words. Use local terms naturally in titles, main headings, meta descriptions, body copy, image alt text, and internal links.
