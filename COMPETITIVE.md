Devil n Dove Website Enhancement Roadmap

For mixed media artists, handmade shops, vintage/collectible sellers, and small creative businesses

The biggest takeaway: Devil n Dove should not look like a generic online store. It should feel like a small creative workshop where every product has a story, materials, process, personality, and proof that real people made it.

Google’s current guidance supports clear titles, clear main headings, helpful product information, descriptive images, and structured product data. Product structured data can help Google understand price, availability, reviews, shipping, return policy, and product variants. Google also warns that confusing or overly prominent multiple headings can make the main page title less clear.

2. Product Page Enhancements

This is one of the most important areas. Baymard’s e-commerce UX research says many product pages still perform poorly, especially on mobile, and users often abandon suitable products because the page does not answer enough questions.

Each Devil n Dove product page should include:

Product identity
One clear H1 product name
Short subtitle explaining what it is
Price
Stock status
Ready-to-ship or made-to-order
Material list
Size / dimensions
Weight, where useful
Colour / finish
Care instructions
Shipping / pickup options
Return or final-sale note
“One of a kind” or “small batch” badge where true
Product story block

Add a section called:

The Story Behind This Piece

Use this for:

Why we made it
What materials were used
Whether it was carved, cast, engraved, printed, painted, polished, or assembled
Whether it came from reclaimed, vintage, or sourced material
Any imperfections that are part of handmade character
Better product highlights

Instead of one long paragraph, use quick scan sections:

Best for: gift, everyday wear, collector, display, cosplay, desk decor, etc.
Materials: polymer clay, stainless steel, resin, wood, stone, metal, etc.
Finish: matte, glossy, sealed, polished, raw, patina
Care: keep dry, avoid chemicals, wipe gently
Handmade note: each piece may vary slightly

Baymard testing found that structured product highlights help users engage with product details more easily than dense descriptions.

3. Product Image & Video Enhancements

This should become a major Devil n Dove advantage.

Google recommends descriptive image filenames, titles, alt text, and responsive images. Google Merchant Center also requires accurate product data and high-quality product images for shopping surfaces.

Each product should ideally have:

Main clean product image
Close-up texture/detail image

Back/side/bottom image
Lifestyle image

5. SEO Enhancements
Keep the one-H1 rule

Continue the current rule: one clear H1 per outward-facing page.

Google says title links can be influenced by the page title, main visual title, heading elements, and other prominent text, and that multiple equally prominent headings can confuse the main title signal.

Page title format

Keep SEO titles under the existing 70-character limit.

Examples:

Handmade Mixed Media Jewelry | Devil n Dove
Polymer Clay Earrings in Southern Ontario | Devil n Dove

13. Accessibility Enhancements

For accessibility, informative images should have useful alt text, while purely decorative images should not be described as if they add meaning. W3C notes that decorative images do not add information to the content and can be handled differently from meaningful images.

Add:

Proper alt text for product images
Empty alt for decorative flourishes
Keyboard-friendly menus
Visible focus states
Strong colour contrast
Larger tap targets on mobile
Avoid text baked into images unless repeated in real text
Captions for videos where possible
Clear form labels and error messages
