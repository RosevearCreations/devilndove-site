Creative Project Workflow • Concept, Process, Operations & Cost

Document the creative process, workshop experiments and maker stories

A product is only one possible result. Capture the idea, materials, tools, time, experiments, mistakes, repairs, finished work, lessons and every intended content output from one authoritative project. Products may be linked or created later, while content-only projects remain separate from storefront inventory and can still generate reviewed content plans.
