ⓘ Help — authoritative records and customer visibility

This workspace does not issue a quote, record a settled deposit, approve a proof on behalf of the customer or start production. Use Custom Requests for quote and payment-request drafts, Custom Work Proofs for actual customer approvals and Manufacturing/Creative Process for job execution. A progress update can be revealed only through an existing active customer order-status token, and only after you explicitly approve its safe wording here. Internal notes, emails, contact details, cost details and private media are never returned by the customer progress API.
