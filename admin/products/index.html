Product correction, deletion, and SKU numbers
Incorrect unused product Load the product into the editor, then use the visible Correct or remove panel. Review linked raw inventory, choose any reservation release or physical material return, then confirm with your password and DELETE PRODUCT.
Product already ordered or referenced Use Archive. It removes the item from active storefront use while preserving order, accounting, and customer history.
Packaging after product facts Use the unified Labeling & Packaging System for soap ribbons, other labels, packaging components, cost, print proof, approvals, and exports.
Automatic numbers Product numbers move forward permanently. Leave SKU blank to receive a unique automatic SKU such as DND-01000; custom SKUs are allowed but must also be unique.
