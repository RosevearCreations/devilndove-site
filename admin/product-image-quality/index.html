How the 100-point image score works

Lighting 20
Exposure and clipped highlights/shadows.
Clarity 20
Edge-energy sharpness heuristic.
Background 15
Border/background uniformity.
Framing 15
Approximate subject occupancy and centering.
Resolution 10
Pixel dimensions; 1200px+ shortest side earns full points.
Colour 10
Channel/white-balance spread.
Artifacts 5
Block-boundary/compression heuristic.
Consistency 5
Aspect-ratio consistency with the Product set.

Automated but explainable: each image also receives a perceptual fingerprint so likely duplicates/near-duplicates can be flagged without modifying or deleting anything. The machine score is evidence, not a claim that a computer understands artistic intent. Reflections, styling, background appropriateness and premium hero appeal remain ideal candidates for an optional vision-assisted review.

How recommendations are used

85–100 Excellent • 70–84 Good • 55–69 Usable / improve • 40–54 Reshoot recommended • below 40 Poor / reshoot.

The highest-scoring distinct image becomes the best current hero candidate. Other distinct images at 70+ become gallery candidates. A perceptual-hash distance of 4 or less is flagged as a probable duplicate; 5–8 is flagged as a possible near duplicate. These are review flags, never automatic deletion.

The Product-set score combines scoring coverage (20), best hero quality (25), average quality (20), distinctness (15) and up to four good/excellent distinct gallery images (20). This lets us rank photography work across the catalog without making subjective AI output a publishing gate.
