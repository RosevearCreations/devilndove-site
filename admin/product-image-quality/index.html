Product Image Quality

Release 448 evidence-based scoring for lighting, clarity, background, framing, resolution, colour balance, artifacts and catalog consistency.

How the 100-point score works

Lighting 20
Exposure and clipped highlights/shadows.
Clarity 20
Edge-energy sharpness heuristic.
Background 15
Border/background uniformity.
Framing 15
Approximate subject occupancy and centering.
Resolution 10
Pixel dimensions; 1200px+ shortest side earns full points.
Colour 10
Channel/white-balance spread.
Artifacts 5
Block-boundary/compression heuristic.
Consistency 5
Aspect-ratio consistency with the Product set.

Important: the machine score is review evidence, not a claim that a computer understands artistic intent. Background suitability, reflections, styling and premium hero readiness can later receive a vision-assisted review. Originals are never altered by scoring.

Recommended interpretation

85–100 Excellent • 70–84 Good • 55–69 Usable / improve • 40–54 Reshoot recommended • below 40 Poor / reshoot. We should use the score to prioritize photography work, not automatically hide a Product.
