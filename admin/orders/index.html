ⓘ What can we safely do here?

The unified seller workspace uses existing Orders, order-detail and fulfilment authorities. Packaging/internal notes and buyer-message drafts save locally only. Live fulfilment and tracking-audit changes require confirmation and stable client_action_id replay protection. Buyer sending, payment/refund execution, carrier calls and accounting posting remain outside this workspace.
