1. Plan required supplies

Enter the amount in usable/usage units, not purchased containers. Planning does not alter any stock.

Stock and usage unit conversion is drawn from Inventory.

ⓘ Why isn't this reserving supplies?

Planning estimates required amounts without claiming or consuming real stock. When ready, use Reserve & Release, which holds stock in canonical Inventory. Before consuming a reserved portion, release that portion first, then record actual usage through Creative Process.

2. Actual receipt reconciliation

After we record real material use in Creative Process, choose its exact receipt here. Linking a receipt does not consume the supply a second time. Scrap is part of the amount already used, not an extra deduction.

ⓘ Returns, partial packages and scrap

Partial packages are converted by Inventory's existing stock-to-usage ratio. To return or correct a real posting, use the original Creative Process reversal action—this BOM will then show that posting as returned. To release an unused reservation, use Materials & Costing. Scrap only classifies a portion of a verified movement; no second deduction happens here. Lot selection is supplier provenance, not a claim that the lot's on-hand balance was depleted.
