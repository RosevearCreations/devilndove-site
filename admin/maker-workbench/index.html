Safety and privacy boundaries

All work stays in its original module. This view cannot create stock movements, record hours, edit orders, approve public media, claim toy/candle safety, authorize customer acceptance, take payment or publish to Etsy or Meta. We remain Canada-first and use CAD wherever cost is shown.
