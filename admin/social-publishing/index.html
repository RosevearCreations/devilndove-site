How a new product becomes a social draft


    

  1. Product is saved as Active and Approved/Published, with a usable product image.
    2. 

  2. When “Create a social draft” is enabled, the app creates one idempotent draft linked to that product.
    3. 

  3. Open the queue to review copy, media, platform choices, UTM link, duplicate warning, and Social Media Privacy Guard result.
    4. 

  4. Explicitly mark the queue item ready + approved only after privacy/public-media review is satisfied.
    5. 

  5. The Build 85 OAuth panel may then show the evidence as ready for a future controlled publication acceptance. It still provides no provider Publish action.
    6. 



Important: Automatic drafting is enabled only by an admin setting. Automatic publishing remains deliberately unavailable because every final post can expose names, addresses, private workshop details, customer information, copyright-protected material, or an unfinished product claim.
