Content Release Board

Turn approved Content Studio plans into reviewable Workshop Journal and website-gallery drafts. Public release remains deliberate: factual copy, public-cleared media, a source-deliverable approval, and an explicit publish action are all required.

Nothing is released by this screen automatically. It only prepares drafts from existing, retained media references. Published stories can be unpublished immediately, and they never remove source photos, videos, R2 objects, or product records.
