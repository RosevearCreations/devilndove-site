Creative Asset Intelligence Platform

Build a searchable, evidence-backed project record from Content Studio media without duplicating files. CAIP scores recorded metadata, protects rights decisions, maps story evidence, and prepares reusable candidates for human review.

Review-first and source-safe. CAIP records references only. It cannot move or delete product/R2 media, grant public rights from scratch, publish a page, render a video, or send content to a platform.
