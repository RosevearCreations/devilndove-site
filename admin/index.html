Full application state, value-added roadmap, desktop/mobile checks, SEO criteria, visual enrichment ideas, and next-step sanity planning.

Website Media & Content Studio

Public-site presentation authority: one page-wide Edit switch, approved image/text placements, metadata, recommended image sizes and the live outstanding-image checklist. It does not manage Creative Project timelines, private CAIP footage or story evidence.

Track every missing image, real-photo requirement, generated editorial asset, rights approval, final URL, alt text, and phone/desktop evidence.
