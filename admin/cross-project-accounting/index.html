Cross-Project Accounting, Revenue & Profitability

Reconcile actual project evidence against source-owned Finance and Accounting without inventing profit or moving money.

ⓘ Help — source boundaries, missing values and financial cautions

Creator project revenue and Finance profitability are distinct source facts, not settled deposits. Stock reservations are not material consumption; posted material use valued at the current inventory unit cost is only an estimate. Creative Process time and work-order time may overlap and are not added together. Production cost evidence can overlap materials and labour and is not automatically added to Finance costs. Linked order payments are cash-collection indicators, not automatically revenue; refunds are applied separately. Taxes are presented separately, and no foreign currency is converted. Do not use this dashboard as a posted ledger, tax return, payroll or year-end close. Review actual records with Accounting and Finance.
