Policy

Made in house

New handmade products require consuming Supply links that resolve to active Inventory and are verified before publication.

Legacy product

Historical handmade products stay legacy_pending until evidence is reconstructed. We never fabricate old consumption.

Outside finished goods

Antiquity, resale and externally finished goods are explicitly exempt from raw-material consumption lineage.

Inventory authority remains unchanged: this workspace never changes stock. Actual consumption continues through site_inventory_movements, production runs and purchase-lot provenance.
