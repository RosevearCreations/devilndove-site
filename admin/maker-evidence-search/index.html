Maker Search & Evidence Finder

Find a real Creative Project, design revision, reviewed recipe version, project-linked workshop tool or serialised finished piece. Results are pointers to their original modules, not copies of evidence or approval decisions.

1. Search source records

ⓘ What should we search first?

Start with Creative Projects if we do not know an ID. Select Search linked evidence on a project result to keep its exact source identity. Other categories need two search characters or a real project scope; tool evidence always needs a project scope. Each search returns at most 20 results with explicit page controls, not a site-wide private-media scan.

2. Original source matches

Ready to search real records after Admin authorization.

ⓘ Can we open original photos or approve finished pieces here?

No. This search does not return original filenames, R2 object keys, private links, customer details, workshop notes or media. Open the original authorized module, select the exact source ID shown, and follow its separate rights, safety, stock and publication controls. A recipe can be superseded; a registered piece is not automatically safe or available to sell.

3. Human evidence and privacy boundaries

Search is authenticated Admin-only and read-only. Private therapeutic work stays private. We do not automatically certify a design, a recipe, a piece, a photograph, a customer request, or an Etsy listing. Source IDs and review states are search hints; genuine owner evidence and external acceptance remain HOLD until reviewed in their own modules.
