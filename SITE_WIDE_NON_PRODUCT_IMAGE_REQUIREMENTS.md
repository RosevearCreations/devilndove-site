# Devil n Dove — Site-Wide Non-Product Image Requirements

**Baseline:** Build 232 Production tree `3fcfd435a8618dc64244f53d0ceca1379878dcdd`.

**Scope:** Images used throughout the customer site and Creator/Admin application that are **not individual Product gallery images**. Product Media remains governed separately.

## Image choice rules

- Use a **real, owned/approved photograph** when the image is meant to prove the workshop, a process, a real event, a real material, real condition, a real tool, or an actual business experience.
- Use a **branded diagram/illustration** when the purpose is to explain a workflow, empty state, system relationship or abstract administrative concept.
- Do not use generated imagery as proof of a real product, workshop condition, event, customer result or manufacturing outcome.
- Public photography should normally have a 3:2 or 4:3 landscape master at 1800px+ long edge plus a responsive WebP derivative.
- Admin diagrams should be SVG where practical and text-light enough to remain readable on phone/tablet.
