Polymer clay earring care

Polymer clay earrings are lightweight and practical for everyday wear, but like other handmade jewelry they benefit from gentle handling and sensible storage.

Wear and storage

Put earrings on after hair spray, perfume, lotions, and makeup have had time to settle. Store pairs where they are not pressed under heavy metal pieces, sharp edges, or loose tools that can scratch the surface.

Cleaning

Start with a soft dry cloth. If the listing permits it, a barely damp soft cloth may be used for a small surface mark, then dry the piece right away. Do not soak handmade earrings or use harsh household cleaners unless the product-specific care note says it is safe.

Handmade variation

Small differences in pattern, marbling, texture, placement, or finish are normal in handmade work. We describe obvious variation and use close-up photos where possible so the piece a buyer receives is clear.

When to contact us

Contact us before trying a repair, glue, solvent, polish, or strong cleaner. A metal finding, resin accent, paint, or sealant can change the right care method for one specific piece.

When in doubt, ask first

If a listing has a special finish, a vintage condition detail, a custom sizing question, or product-specific safety notes, the individual listing and direct message take priority over this general guide.
